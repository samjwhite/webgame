Pixel art for the 2D top-down/side-view game "Літо в селі" (Summer in the Village), set in the village of Makitryne, Poltava region, Ukraine, summer 2003.
Style: detailed, warm, cosy pixel art in the spirit of "Cast n Chill" and Stardew Valley, with visible pixels, soft dithering, a limited earthy palette (warm ochres, dusty greens, faded blues, whitewash, rust), gentle late-afternoon light and dark outlines on characters and props.
Era and place: post-Soviet Ukrainian village under Kuchma, lived-in and weathered but cared for — whitewashed houses with blue window frames, wavy asbestos slate roofs, chipped plaster showing brick, wooden picket fences, yellow gas pipes, concrete bus stops with mosaics, Soviet-era vehicles and signs in Ukrainian.
Avoid: anime or Japanese village styling, pristine or glossy surfaces, modern objects or brands after 2003, photorealism, smooth vector shading, blurry anti-aliased edges, text other than short Ukrainian signs.
For sprites and sprite sheets: plain flat dark background (#26222f) or transparent background, every object or frame clearly separated with empty space around it, same scale and ground line for all frames, no labels or captions unless asked.
